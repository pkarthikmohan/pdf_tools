Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using our free online PDF tools.

Are my files safe and private? ▾

Yes, completely. Unlike traditional online PDF editors that send your sensitive documents to remote servers, our PDF tools process everything locally on your computer via client-side JavaScript. Your files never leave your device.

How do I merge multiple PDF files? ▾

Select the Merge PDF tool, drag and drop or browse to select your PDF documents, rearrange the files into your preferred order, and click "Merge PDFs" to instantly download the combined document.

How can I compress a PDF without losing quality? ▾

Click Compress PDF, select your document, and pick your preferred compression level (Extreme, Recommended, or High Quality). Our tool reduces redundant metadata and optimizes imagery to save disk space while preserving text readability.

Can I convert images and Word files to PDF? ▾

Yes! You can convert JPG, PNG, and WebP images directly into a PDF, or convert Word (DOCX) files to clean, shareable PDFs with one click.

Is there any file size limit or cost? ▾

No. PDF Tools is 100% free and open to everyone without daily task limits, subscriptions, or watermarks.
